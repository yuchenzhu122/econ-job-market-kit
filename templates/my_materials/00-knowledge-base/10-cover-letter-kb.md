# 10 · Cover letter paragraphs

make_letter.py reads every "## key" section below as one paragraph (plain text; & and quotes
are handled for you). {position}, {dept}, {employer} and {letter_writers} are filled in per letter.
Only the fit paragraph (and, for industry letters, the middle --body) is new for each letter.

Academic, research template: intro, jmp, other_research, teaching, closing
Academic, teaching template: intro_teaching, teaching_approach, teaching_evidence, research_brief, closing_teaching
Industry: industry_intro, industry_closing

## intro
I am writing to apply for the {position} position in the {dept} at {employer}. ...

## industry_intro
I am writing to apply for the {position} role at {employer}. I am completing a Ph.D. in Economics at ...

## industry_closing
I would welcome the chance to discuss how I could contribute to your team. Thank you for your time and consideration.
